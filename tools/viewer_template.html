TinyCandle Light

ATtiny85 in a machined-pin socket on a 9×9 mini protoboard, with an 8-LED WS2812B ring centred over the chip. The light mounts on a jar lid and the glass jar turns upside down over it as the diffuser.

    The 0.1µF cap lies flat inside the socket window. The ATtiny85 pulls straight out of the socket for reflashing. The ring sits on two white nylon standoffs; its holes are rotated to land on G1 and B8, the only free grid holes the right distance apart. Screw heads must be under Ø3.3 to clear the LEDs. The jar, lid, ring pad order and mounting hole are placeholders until measured.
